What your CRM website includes

        
Everything runs on free Unyson+ features inside WordPress. There are no extra CRM subscriptions, and your leads stay in your own database.

      

      

        
Property listings
A Properties post type with price, beds, baths, area, map, gallery and virtual tour, plus filters by type, location and features.

        
Lead capture forms
Contact, showing request, home valuation, buyer consultation and listing alerts. Every submission is stored as an entry, not only emailed.

        
Contacts and pipeline
Leads go into the built-in CRM with tags for each pipeline stage, so you can see who is new, who has a showing booked and who has closed.

        
Email follow-up
Welcome emails, new listing announcements and market updates sent to lists and saved segments, with double opt-in and unsubscribe links.

        
Search engine ready
Clean titles and descriptions, Open Graph tags, structured data and a sitemap from the SEO extension.

        
Privacy built in
Consent checkboxes on forms, and contacts covered by WordPress's own personal data export and erase tools.

      

    

  


  

    

      
A note on MLS and IDX

      
No website tool can pull MLS listings on its own. MLS data comes through an IDX agreement between you, your brokerage and your MLS, usually delivered by an IDX provider. If you have one, choose My IDX provider above and the agent will install that provider's official plugin or embed. Your lead forms and CRM work the same either way. If you do not have one yet, start with manual listings; you can add IDX later without rebuilding the site.

    

  


  

    

      
Questions

      
Is the CRM website generator really free?
Yes. The generator, the Unyson+ plugin and theme, and the AI Dev Kit are free. You only pay for your own hosting and, if you use one, your AI agent subscription.

      
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You fill in a form and copy the result. Your AI agent does the building. After it is built, the site is edited in the WordPress admin with a visual page builder.

      
Which AI agents can use the prompt?
Any agent that can read files and run commands on the machine or server hosting WordPress, such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex. Chat-only assistants can follow the plan, but you will have to carry out the steps yourself.

      
Does it connect to the MLS or include IDX?
MLS data requires your own IDX agreement through your brokerage and MLS. The generator supports two routes: manual listings you add yourself, or an IDX provider you already use, whose plugin or widget the agent installs. Leads from either route go into the same CRM.

      
Is the result a WordPress site?
Yes. It is a normal WordPress site built with the free Unyson+ framework, so you own it, can host it anywhere and can edit everything in the admin.

      
Can I use it for industries other than real estate?
Real estate is the first preset. More industries are on the roadmap, and the site spec format is the same for all of them.
