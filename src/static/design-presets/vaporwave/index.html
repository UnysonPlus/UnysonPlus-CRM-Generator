// What the preset decides

Three rules

01

The void on every neon

White on the cyan is one and a quarter to one — the two colours are nearly the same luminance. The background colour on it is sixteen.

02

Borders have width

A neon tube is a physical object with a diameter. A one-pixel line in the same colour is a divider pretending to be one, and the difference is immediately visible.

03

The black is purple

Nine, zero, twenty. A neutral black makes the neon read as paint on a surface; the purple tint makes it read as light in a space.

// Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings, and a generated site carries the void, the two neons, Orbitron over Share Tech Mono, the zero radius and the tube-width borders. The receding grid, the gradient sun and the glass panels are markup, so a section that wants them still asks for them.
Why is the button text dark?
Because white on the cyan measures one and a quarter to one — the two are nearly the same luminance, so the label is simply not there. The void colour on it is sixteen. A neon surface feels dark because it is saturated, and it is not.
Why is nothing rounded?
Because the whole visual language is angular — a grid, a horizon, a tube. One rounded corner and the page stops being a space and becomes a card layout with bright colours.
