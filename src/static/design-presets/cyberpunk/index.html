// What the preset decides

Three rules

01

The void on every fill

The ink on the primary neon is 1.02 to one — identical luminance, so the text is simply not there. A neon element carries the background colour instead, at 14.73.

02

Glow is not contrast

A halo reads as brightness to the eye and as nothing to a contrast checker. Every glow here sits on a pairing that already passes without it.

03

The static state is the style

The chamfer and the colour split carry the whole register with zero frames of animation, so reduced motion removes nothing. Nothing on this page flickers on its own.

// Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings; a generated site inherits the palette, the monospace stacks and the zero radius. The chamfered clip-paths, the scanline layer and the colour split are still being folded into the preset and are not all emitted automatically yet.
Why does nothing glitch?
Because a flicker is exactly what reduced motion exists to suppress, and a style that only works while animating does not work. The split is drawn statically, so the effect is present in a screenshot, in print, and for a visitor who has asked for stillness.
Why is the secondary grey not the one in the specification?
Because that grey measures 4.09 to one on the void, 3.85 on a card and 3.46 on the chrome — it fails on every surface the specification itself defines. The value here is #9AA3B2 at 7.76.
