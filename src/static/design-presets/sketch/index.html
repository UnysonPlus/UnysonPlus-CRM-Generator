What the preset decides

Three rules

1

The marker had to come down

Three point one six as text, three point two seven under white, four point two one under the pencil. It fails in every direction — a colour picked for how it looks and never measured.

2

No two corners match

Four radius values per element, swapping on hover so the line appears redrawn. One uniform radius and this is an ordinary card with a funny font.

3

Rotate the box, not the words

A container tilts a degree; its contents counter-rotate so they sit straight. Tilted body text is harder to read and buys nothing.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings, and a generated site carries the paper, the pencil ink, the two handwriting faces, the larger body size and the two-pixel border with its irregular radius. The rotations, the dashed rules and the ruled feint are markup, so a section that wants them still asks for them.
Why is the red darker than the specification?
Because the specified red is the rare colour that fails in all three directions at once. Keeping the saturation and lowering the lightness holds the marker quality and moves it to four and a half to one in both of the directions that matter.
Why is the body text so large?
Because a handwriting face at sixteen pixels is harder work than a text face at the same size — the letterforms are less regular, so the eye does more. A point or two up costs nothing and is the difference between charming and tiring.
