What the preset decides

Three rules

I

Sage is a surface

The specification calls it the primary colour, and it fails as text, under white and under the forest ink. Its own primary button is forest with white at nearly twelve to one — which is the answer hiding in the question.

II

Every neutral is warm

Paper, stone, clay and panel are all tinted. Drop one cool grey in among them and it reads as a different stylesheet rather than as a neutral.

III

The paper has tooth

A fractal grain at three and a half per cent. Without it the warm off-white is a flat tint; with it the page is a sheet, which is the whole premise.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings, and a generated site carries the paper, the forest ink, the pills, the stone borders and the type pairing. The leaf shapes, the herbarium plate and the paper grain are markup, so a section that wants them still asks for them.
Why is sage not the button colour?
Because it fails in all three directions — as text on the paper, under white and under the forest ink. It is a beautiful surface and a poor role, and the specification's own primary button already used forest, which is the giveaway.
Why pills rather than rounded rectangles?
Because nothing in a herbarium has a square corner. A pill reads as grown rather than cut, which is the one shape decision that carries the whole register.
