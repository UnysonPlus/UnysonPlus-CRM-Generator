Built from light, not from fills.

What the preset decides

A system of surfaces

The canvas is never a flat fill

A radial gradient for vertical depth, noise so a wide dark screen does not band, a 64 px grid for precision, and three slow-drifting pools of light. Remove them and what is left is an ordinary dark mode — the layering is the style, not the darkness.

One accent

Indigo, for interaction and almost nothing else. An accent used decoratively reads as a theme; used sparingly it reads as light.

Shadows in layers

A hairline, a soft diffuse, an ambient darkness — and a glow on the accent. One shadow looks pasted on; three read as a surface sitting in space.

Small, swift motion

200–300 ms, expo-out, four to eight pixels. Never a bounce.

Interaction

Depth you can point at

Hover a card. A 300 px pool of light follows the cursor across its surface, the border brightens by four percent, and the whole thing lifts four pixels. Each change is small enough to feel like a response rather than an animation.

Cursor-tracked light

A radial gradient positioned from the pointer, at fifteen percent opacity. It is the one effect that makes the surface feel physical.

A lit top edge

A single bright pixel along the top of every raised surface, fading out at both ends. Almost invisible, and the page looks cheap without it.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings; a generated site inherits the palette, the type and the surfaces. The atmosphere on this page — the drifting light, the cursor-tracked glow, the layered shadows — is still being folded into the preset and is not all emitted automatically yet.
Is a dark page like this accessible?
With one caveat worth stating. Body text sits at about 15:1 and muted text at 6.3:1, both comfortable. But the accent is split in two: the indigo fill carries white at 4.7:1 and passes, while that same indigo as text on the canvas is only 4.32:1 and fails — so accent type uses a brighter variant at 4.84:1. Using one value for both is the quiet failure in this palette.
What happens with reduced motion?
The drifting light stops outright rather than merely running faster, and the lift and scale are removed. Nothing on the page depends on movement to be understood.
