What the preset decides

Three rules

01

The card is lighter than the page

Which makes it the harder surface, not the easier one. The spec's secondary grey is 4.09 to one on the canvas and 3.57 inside a card — it fails in both places, and worse where you would check last.

02

Glow is not contrast

The ambient orbs and the halo on a hovered button are the signature of the style and they are decoration. Every pairing here passes before any glow is applied to it.

03

The space is the style

Section padding runs to 176px. Tightening it is the quickest way to lose the calm, and it costs nothing to keep — unlike a colour, space is free.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a mock-up or the real thing?
A specimen, hand-built to the preset's specification so the style can be seen whole rather than as a thumbnail. The preset itself is a set of WordPress Theme Settings, and a generated site carries the palette, the type stack and scale, the soft radius, the four surfaces and the 8% borders. The glass fills, the ambient orbs and the backdrop blur are markup rather than stored values, so a section that wants them still asks for them.
Why is the button text dark?
Because white on the amber is 2.15 to one. An amber button reads as a dark object — it is saturated and warm — and it is not: the canvas colour on it is 9.20. The specification gets this right and it is still the first thing people change.
Why only one accent?
Because the contrast of the whole design is warm against cool. Add a second accent and the amber stops being the ember in the dark and becomes one of two colours in a theme, which is a different and much more ordinary thing.
